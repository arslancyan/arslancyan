About

Digital art, characters & visual storytelling.

I'm ARSLANCYAN, a digital artist creating illustrations, character art, memes and animation for online communities, brands and independent projects.

My work combines expressive characters, clean line art, humor and bold color to create visuals that are easy to recognize and fun to remember.

All of my artwork is created using ibisPaint X on a Huawei tablet, drawn entirely with my finger.
